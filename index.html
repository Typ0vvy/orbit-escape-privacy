Orbit Escape - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Orbit Escape ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by Marcin Kopec ("the developer").

Data we collect

The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts and does not require an internet connection to play.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, accessibility options) and your level progress are stored only locally on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game or reset progress in the game settings.

Device features

The game uses the vibration motor and the accelerometer (for a subtle background effect) only on your device. No sensor data leaves your device.

Advertising

A future version of the game may show full-screen advertisements provided by Google AdMob after several completed levels. If advertising is enabled, the advertising provider may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID in accordance with its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
